Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about instruments, plans, orders, and support.

General

Who is Hiraya Musiko for?

We create instruments and accessories designed for beginner, intermediate, and performing musicians who appreciate Filipino-inspired sound and quality.

Products

Do you offer acoustic and electric options?

Yes. Our product range includes acoustic guitars, electric guitars, keyboards, drums, and accessories.

Orders

How do I place an order?

Choose a product, add it to cart, proceed to checkout, and complete the demo order form.

Payment

What payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery, GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, and Credit / Debit Card are shown as demo payment options.

Delivery

How long will delivery take?

Timing depends on the product, region, and stock availability. Please refer to the product detail page and contact us for the latest timing.

Support

Can I ask for help choosing an instrument?

Yes. Please use the contact form or consultation page and we will guide you based on your playing needs.
