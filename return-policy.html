Order Support

Return Policy

Original demo return process for the Hiraya Musiko storefront.

Eligibility

When returns can be requested

Returns may be considered for items that are damaged, defective, or materially different from the product description at the time of delivery. Requests should be submitted promptly and accompanied by proof of purchase and photos where relevant.

Process

What to do

  1. Contact our support team with your order number and details.
  2. Provide evidence such as photos or a description of the issue.
  3. Wait for approval and follow the return instructions provided.
  4. Ship the item back in the original packaging where possible.

Inspection

Return review

Items are inspected before approval to confirm whether the issue is eligible for return or exchange.

Refunds

Payment outcomes

Refunds, credits, or replacements will depend on the final review, product condition, and order status.

Shipping

Return shipping

Return shipping may be the customer's responsibility unless the issue is due to a defect or fulfillment error.
