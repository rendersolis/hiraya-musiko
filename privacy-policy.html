Privacy Policy

How Hiraya Musiko handles demo account and browsing data.

Website information

What we collect

We may collect basic contact information, order details, and account-related details when users sign up, log in, or place a demo order.

Some browsing preferences and cart data are stored in the browser using localStorage to support the demo storefront experience.

Use of data

Why we use it

Data may be used to support account access, contact communication, order processing, and site functionality such as cart state, wishlist tracking, and product selection.

Cookies

Browser storage

This demo site may use local browser storage to restore cart, wishlist, and user activity. It does not process real payment data.

Choices

Control

Users may choose to clear browser data to remove stored demo preferences and local cart information.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact us through the customer support form or service page.
