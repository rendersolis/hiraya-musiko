Our Story

Made for Filipino musicians

Hiraya Instruments was built around a simple idea: Filipino creativity deserves to be heard in every instrument we make. Our guitars, ukuleles, and other instruments are designed with the spirit of local artistry, modern performance, and everyday playability in mind.

We draw inspiration from the rhythm of the islands, the warmth of local traditions, and the energy of musicians who perform in homes, studios, cafés, and stages across the Philippines.

Every Hiraya piece is shaped to feel personal, expressive, and dependable—made for players who want musical instruments that feel connected to their roots while still meeting modern standards.

The Hiraya approach

Crafted for sound, identity, and performance

At Hiraya Instruments, we believe every musician deserves an instrument that reflects both skill and spirit. We want our guitars and accessories to feel at home in every Filipino performance space—from intimate home practice to lively community gigs.

Our designs blend musical clarity with the warmth of local creativity, helping players discover an instrument that feels authentic, inspiring, and easy to love.

This is the heart of our work: thoughtful design, natural tone, and instruments made to be played with pride.
